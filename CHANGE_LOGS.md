# Change Log: Web App

What changed in the aMACEzing Sales web app, newest first. Versions use the same format as the Android app: **YY.M.D.N**, which is the year, month and day of the release (Manila time) and `N`, a counter for releases on the same day that starts at 1. For example, 26.11.3.2 is the second release of 3 November 2026.

---

## 26.11.4.1

- **Change Log:** this page, under **Settings > Change Log**. It is loaded from the public repository, so new entries appear without a new build.

## 26.11.3.3

- **Net Sales is always shown** on the Home dashboard. Estimated Profit and Margin stay hidden while **Mask Profit Values** is on.

## 26.11.3.2

- **Sales & Profit Trend chart:** the bars follow the selected period (months for This Year, All Time and long custom ranges; days for Today, This Week, This Month and short custom ranges), so the old six-month limit is gone.
- **Scrolling and labels:** wide charts scroll sideways and open on the latest bars, and every bar has a short amount above it (12.4k).
- **Comparison:** an outlined bar beside each bar shows the earlier period (last year, last month, last week, yesterday or the range before a custom one), with the change in net sales next to the title.
- **Very tall bars** are shortened and marked with a gap, so the other bars stay readable.
- **Profit hidden:** while profit is masked, the chart says so and scales from net sales only.

## 26.11.3.1

- **PDF Watermark switch** in Settings: turn the "Generated by aMACEzing Sales Monitoring System" line on quotation and receipt PDFs on or off. It is on by default and synced with your account, so it applies in the Android app too.

## Earlier releases

Built before the versions above, newest first. They have no recorded dates, so they are listed without a version number.

### Feature parity with the Android app

The web app now matches the Android app, apart from the fingerprint lock, Duplicate order, Check for Updates and Sync to Cloud.

- **Customers:** add, edit and delete customers, with their order history.
- **Reports** with CSV export.
- **Works offline** and can be installed as an app (on iPhone, the button shows the Add to Home Screen steps).
- **Sign-in on iPhone** works from the home screen and when pop-ups are blocked.
- **Privacy notice** and a switch for usage analytics, which only start after you agree.
- **Home checklist** with action buttons.
- **Live updates:** changes made in the Android app or on the web show up on the other side while it is open.

### Editing orders

- **Edit orders:** change items, discount and remarks, or cancel an order. Payments stay as they are.

### Profile, import and wide screens

- **Profile & Branding** page: business identity, logo, branding fields and preferences, synced with the app.
- **Import & Export:** import orders and products from CSV or Excel, with downloadable templates.
- **Wide screens:** the layout widens on tablets and big screens, with a side navigation rail from 900 px.

### Settings and About

- **Settings:** add and edit businesses (colour, icon, profit method), archive or delete them, and **Mask Profit Values**.
- **About** page with the developer name and the Buy me a coffee QR, which can be downloaded.
- Businesses and settings sync both ways with the Android app.

### PDFs

- **PDFs** for quotations and receipts, with the same layout as the Android app and a share button on phones.

### Orders (quotations)

- **Orders (quotations):** create, edit, change status, convert to an order and delete, synced both ways with the Android app.

### Writing data

- **Writing data:** add and edit products, create orders and record payments, synced both ways with the Android app.

### Android look and feel

- **Android look and feel:** dark theme, top bar, bottom navigation, chips, cards and dialogs.
- Read-only **Home, Sales, Inventory, Customers and Profile** screens, and the in-app **User Guide**.
- The sign-in page mirrors the Android sign-in page, and the new logo is used everywhere.

### Google sign-in

- **Google sign-in**, and your businesses, products, customers and orders load from the same account and database as the Android app.

### First release

- **First release:** the web app is hosted on GitHub Pages and connected to the same Firebase project as the Android app.
