Manage your business. Grow your sales.

This guide explains what aMACEzing Sales can do and how to use each part of the app. You can open it any time inside the app by tapping the **?** icon at the top right.

## Getting started

### Installing and updating

- Download the APK from the Releases page of this repository and install it. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser or file manager once.
- Every time the app starts it checks for a newer version before showing anything else.
- If a newer version exists, you must install it to continue. Tap **Update now**, wait for the download, then confirm the install.
- If the app can't check for updates (for example, no internet), or the update is cancelled, you will see "You are using an unsupported version. Update first to proceed." Connect to the internet and tap **Try again**.

### Signing in

1. Tap **Sign in with Google** and choose your account.
2. A loading screen ("Syncing your data") appears while the app loads your cloud backup. On a new phone this restores your businesses, products, customers, orders and quotations.
3. When loading finishes, the Home screen appears.

### First-time checklist

The Home screen shows three steps until you finish them:

1. **Add your first business** (Settings).
2. **Add your products or services** (Inventory).
3. **Record your first sale** (Sales).

You need at least one business before you can create orders, quotations or products.

## Businesses

A business is a group for your orders, products and quotations, for example "Engraving" or "Printing". You can run several businesses in one app and filter by business almost everywhere.

### Adding or editing a business

Open **Settings**, then **Businesses**, and add or edit one. Each business has:

- **Business name**
- **Order number prefix:** used to number orders, for example ENG gives ENG-2026-0001.
- **Color and icon:** shown on chips and badges so you can tell businesses apart.
- **Active:** inactive businesses are hidden from new orders.
- **How profit is estimated:**
  - **Use unit costs:** profit is net sales minus the total unit cost of the items.
  - **Fixed margin %:** profit is net sales times the margin you set. You can also tick **Use actual costs when available** to use real unit costs whenever an order has them.

### Deleting a business

The app asks you to confirm before deleting a business. Check that you no longer need its orders and products first.

### Mask profit values

In **Settings**, turn on **Mask Profit Values** to hide profit amounts on screen (they show as dots). Useful when showing your phone to customers or staff.

## Inventory (products)

The **Inventory** tab lists the products and services you sell, for each business.

### Adding a product

Tap the add button, then fill in:

- **Product name** (required)
- **Selling price** (required, 0 or more)
- **Unit cost** (optional). It is used to estimate profit. If you leave it blank it is saved as 0.
- **Active in catalog:** turn this off to keep a product in your list but mark it inactive.

You will see an estimated unit margin on the card, so you can check that your price covers your cost.

### Editing and deleting

Use **Edit** or **Delete** on a product card. Deleting asks you to confirm.

## Customers

Open **Customers** from your Profile page. The directory shows each customer with their orders, lifetime sales, total paid and balance.

- **Search** by name, phone or email.
- Tap a customer to see their order history.
- Customers are created automatically when you save an order with a new customer name, so you rarely need to add them by hand.

## Orders (quotations)

The **Orders** tab is where you prepare price quotations for customers before they buy.

### Creating a quotation

1. Tap the add button.
2. Choose the **business division**.
3. Enter the customer's details: name or company (required), contact number, email and address. Tap **Select Existing** to reuse a customer.
4. Add the **products and services**: tap **Pick Catalog** to choose from your inventory or **Add Custom Item** for something else. Set the quantity and unit price.
5. Set a **discount** (percent or fixed amount) and the **validity** in days.
6. Add project scope or remarks if you want, then tap **Generate Quotation**.

The quotation is saved and a PDF is created that you can view, share or print.

### Statuses and actions

A quotation moves through Draft, Sent to Client, Accepted, Declined and Converted to Order. On each quotation card you can:

- **PDF:** open the quotation PDF again.
- **Convert:** turn the quotation into a sales order (the quotation is then marked Converted).
- **Edit** or **Delete.**

## Sales (orders and payments)

The **Sales** tab lists your orders. Use it to record sales, take payments and issue receipts.

### Recording a new order

1. Tap the add button.
2. Pick the **business** and enter the **customer name** (required).
3. Check the **order date**.
4. Tap **Add Product** for each line, then set the quantity and unit price.
5. Apply a **discount** if needed, as a percentage or a fixed peso amount.
6. Enter the **initial payment received** (if any) and any payment notes or remarks.
7. The totals update as you type: gross sales, discount, net sales and the outstanding balance. Tap **Create Order** to save.

### Payment status

- **Pending:** nothing paid yet.
- **Partially Paid:** some money received, a balance remains.
- **Paid:** fully paid.
- **Cancelled:** the order was cancelled.

### Working with an order

Tap an order to open its details. From there you can:

- **Receipt:** create a receipt PDF to share or print.
- **Duplicate:** copy the order to start a similar one quickly.
- **Pay:** record a payment. Enter the amount, choose the payment method (for example Cash, GCash, Bank Transfer or Check), and add a reference number or notes if you like. The balance and status update automatically.
- **Edit** or **Delete** the order.

### Finding orders

- Search by order number, customer or product.
- Filter by payment status and by business.
- Choose the **period** (All Time, Today, This Week, This Month, This Year or a Custom Range) with the chips under the business chips. The list only shows orders inside that period. The period is shared with Home and Reports, so changing it on one page changes it on the others.
- Sort by newest date or by highest net sales.

## Home dashboard

Home gives you a live summary of your business.

- **Business chips:** view everything together, or one business at a time.
- **Period chips:** All Time, Today, This Week, This Month, This Year or a **Custom Range** you pick.
- **Summary cards:**
  - Gross Sales, Discounts, Average Order and your Top Customer.
  - Net Sales and Estimated Profit (with the profit margin).
  - Collected Cash and Outstanding receivables.
- **Charts:** monthly sales and profit trend, business comparison, product sales breakdown, and paid versus unpaid.
- **Outstanding Receivables:** orders with a balance, with quick **Details** and **Pay** buttons.
- **Recent Transactions:** your latest orders. Tap one to open it.

## Import and export

Open **Data Import** from your Profile page. Use the switch at the top to choose **Orders** or **Products**.

### Importing products

1. Choose the **business**. Every product in the file is added to that business.
2. Download a **CSV or Excel template** and fill it in, one product per row.
3. Tap **Choose CSV or Excel file** and pick your file.
4. Review the results: how many are new, how many already exist, and any problems listed by row.
5. Tap **Import** to confirm. Valid products are saved and uploaded to your cloud backup.

Columns:

- **Product Name** (required)
- **Selling Price** (required, a number of 0 or more)
- **Unit Cost** (optional, 0 or more; blank is saved as 0)
- **Active** (optional, Yes or No; blank means Yes)

Notes:

- Products with the same name already in that business are skipped. Turn on **Update existing products** to replace their price, cost and status instead.
- A name repeated inside the file is flagged, and only its first row is imported.
- Rows with problems are skipped; the rest are still imported.

### Importing orders

Download the orders template and fill in one row per product line. The required columns are Order Date, Customer, Business, Product, Quantity and Unit Price. Optional columns are Order Ref, Unit Cost, Discount %, Amount Paid, Payment Method and Remarks. Rows that share the same Order Ref become one order. You see a preview and any problems before anything is saved.

### Exporting orders

Use **Export** on the same screen to save your orders as a CSV file.

## Profile and branding

Open **Profile** from your photo at the top right.

- **Authenticated identity:** your Google account, managed by Google.
- **Business Branding & Logo:** your business name, tagline, owner, contact number, email, address, website, social page and an optional TIN. Add your logo here.
- **Document preferences:** default quotation terms, payment instructions, receipt footer and the default validity in days.
- **Show on documents:** choose whether your logo and contact details appear on quotations and receipts.

Tap **Save Profile** to apply. If you leave with unsaved changes, the app asks whether to keep editing or discard.

## Cloud backup and sync

Your data is stored on your phone and backed up to your own account in the cloud.

- **When you sign in:** a loading screen appears while your backup is restored. If the connection is slow, the app continues with the data on your phone and syncs later.
- **Sync to Cloud:** on the **About** page, tap this to upload everything now. The status (Cloud Synced, Syncing to Cloud, Saved Locally (Offline) or Sync Error) is shown there and on your Profile.
- **Working offline:** you can keep recording sales without internet. Changes are saved on the phone and uploaded when you are back online.
- **Logging out:** the app first backs up your data to the cloud, then clears this phone. Signing in again restores it. If the backup can't finish, the app tells you why before logging out.

> Tip: tap **Sync to Cloud** after a busy day, before changing phones.

## Questions and troubleshooting

### "You are using an unsupported version"

The app couldn't confirm it is up to date, or an update was cancelled. Check your internet connection and tap **Try again**. If an update is offered, tap **Update now** and finish the install.

### The install is blocked

Android asks you to allow installs from this app once. Turn on **Allow from this source** in the settings screen that opens, go back, and tap **Install**.

### My data isn't showing after signing in

Wait for the "Syncing your data" screen to finish. If you were offline, connect to the internet, then open **About** and tap **Sync to Cloud**.

### I can't create an order, quotation or product

Add a business first: **Settings**, then **Businesses**.

### My import has problems

Read the list of problems under the summary. Each one names the row and what to fix. Fix the file and choose it again.

### The guide won't load

The guide is loaded from the internet. Check your connection and tap the refresh icon. If you are offline, the app shows the last saved copy when it has one.
