# aMACEzing Sales: Features & User Guide

Manage your business. Grow your sales.

This guide explains what aMACEzing Sales can do and how to use each part of it. It covers the Android app and the web version, which share the same data. You can open it any time by tapping the **?** icon at the top right.

> Tip: set things up in this order: sign in, add your business, add your products, then record your first sale. The Home screen shows a checklist until all three are done, and each open step has a button that takes you straight there.

## Getting started

### Installing and updating

- Download the APK from the Releases page of this repository and install it. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser or file manager once.
- Every time the app starts it checks for a newer version before showing anything else.
- If a newer version exists, you must install it to continue. Tap **Update now**, wait for the download, then confirm the install.
- If the app can't check for updates (for example, no internet), or the update is cancelled, you will see "You are using an unsupported version. Update first to proceed." Connect to the internet and tap **Try again**.

### Signing in

1. Tap **Sign in with Google** and choose your account.
2. A loading screen ("Syncing your data") appears while the app loads your cloud backup. On a new phone this restores your businesses, products, customers, orders and quotations.
3. When loading finishes, the Home screen appears.

### First-time checklist

The Home screen shows three steps until you finish them:

1. **Add your first business** (Settings).
2. **Add your products or services** (Inventory).
3. **Record your first sale** (Sales).

Each step that is not done yet has a button (Add business, Add products, New sale) that opens the right place. The second and third buttons wait until you have a business.

Already have past orders in a spreadsheet? Use **Data Import** from your Profile page to bring them in instead of typing them again.

You need at least one business before you can create orders, quotations or products.

## Finding your way around

- **Bottom bar:** Home, Orders, Sales, Inventory and Settings.
- **Top bar:** the **?** icon opens this guide, and your profile photo opens your Profile. In a web browser that can install the app, an **install** icon appears next to the **?** icon.
- **Profile page:** your account, business branding, shortcuts to Customers, Sales Reports and Data Import, the biometric lock and log out. The About page is reachable from Settings.

- **Big screens:** on a tablet or computer the web version widens, shows more cards side by side, and puts the main menu in a bar down the left side.

## Businesses

A business is a group for your orders, products and quotations, for example "Engraving" or "Printing". You can run several businesses in one app and filter by business almost everywhere.

### Adding or editing a business

Open **Settings**, then **Businesses**, and add or edit one. Each business has:

- **Business name**
- **Order number prefix:** used to number orders, for example ENG gives ENG-2026-0001 in the app. Orders made on the web get a W in the number (ENG-2026-W0001), so web and phone numbers can never clash.
- **Color and icon:** shown on chips and badges so you can tell businesses apart.
- **Active:** inactive businesses are hidden from new orders.
- **How profit is estimated:**
  - **Use unit costs:** profit is net sales minus the total unit cost of the items.
  - **Fixed margin %:** profit is net sales times the margin you set. You can also tick **Use actual costs when available** to use real unit costs whenever an order has them.

### Deleting a business

The app asks you to confirm before deleting a business. Check that you no longer need its orders and products first.

### Mask profit values

In **Settings**, turn on **Mask Profit Values** to hide profit amounts on screen (they show as dots). Useful when showing your phone to customers or staff.

### PDF watermark

In **Settings**, **PDF Watermark** controls the small line "Generated by aMACEzing Sales Monitoring System" at the bottom of quotation and receipt PDFs. It is on by default. Turn it off and new PDFs leave that line out. The choice is saved with your account, so it applies in the app and on the web.

## Inventory (products)

The **Inventory** tab lists the products and services you sell, for each business.

### Adding a product

Tap the add button, then fill in:

- **Product name** (required)
- **Selling price** (required, 0 or more)
- **Unit cost** (optional). It is used to estimate profit. If you leave it blank it is saved as 0.
- **Active in catalog:** turn this off to keep a product in your list but mark it inactive.

You will see an estimated unit margin on the card, so you can check that your price covers your cost.

### Editing and deleting

Use **Edit** or **Delete** on a product card. Deleting asks you to confirm.

## Customers

Open **Customers** from your Profile page. The directory shows each customer with their orders, lifetime sales, total paid and balance.

- **Search** by name, phone or email.
- Tap a customer to see their order history.
- Use **Add** to create a customer with a name (required), contact number, email and notes. Use **Edit** or **Delete** on a customer card.
- Customers are created automatically when you save an order with a new customer name, so you rarely need to add them by hand.
- Renaming a customer also changes the name on their saved orders and quotations.
- Open a customer's order history and tap **Statement PDF** (the PDF icon in the app) for a statement of account: all their orders (cancelled ones left out), what was paid and the balance due.
- Deleting a customer keeps their orders in your records.

## Orders (quotations)

The **Orders** tab is where you prepare price quotations for customers before they buy.

### Creating a quotation

1. Tap the add button.
2. Choose the **business division**.
3. Enter the customer's details: name or company (required), contact number, email and address. Tap **Select Existing** to reuse a customer.
4. Add the **products and services**: tap **Pick Catalog** to choose from your inventory or **Add Custom Item** for something else. Set the quantity and unit price.
5. Set a **discount** (percent or fixed amount) and the **validity** in days.
6. Add project scope or remarks if you want, then tap **Generate Quotation**.

The quotation is saved and a PDF is created that you can view, share or print.

### Statuses and actions

A quotation moves through Draft, Sent to Client, Accepted, Declined and Converted to Order. On each quotation card you can:

- **PDF:** open the quotation PDF again.
- **Convert:** turn the quotation into a sales order (the quotation is then marked Converted).
- **Edit** or **Delete.**

## Sales (orders and payments)

The **Sales** tab lists your orders. Use it to record sales, take payments and issue receipts.

### Recording a new order

1. Tap the add button.
2. Pick the **business** and enter the **customer name** (required).
3. Check the **order date**.
4. Tap **Add Product** for each line, then set the quantity and unit price.
5. Apply a **discount** if needed, as a percentage or a fixed peso amount.
6. Enter the **initial payment received** (if any) and any payment notes or remarks.
7. The totals update as you type: gross sales, discount, net sales and the outstanding balance. Tap **Create Order** to save.

### Payment status

- **Pending:** nothing paid yet.
- **Partially Paid:** some money received, a balance remains.
- **Paid:** fully paid.
- **Cancelled:** the order was cancelled.

### Working with an order

Tap an order to open its details. From there you can:

- **Receipt:** create a receipt PDF to share or print.
- **Duplicate:** create a new order with the same customer, items and discount, dated today, with no payment yet. The remarks say which order it was copied from.
- **Pay:** record a payment. Enter the amount, choose the payment method (for example Cash, GCash, Bank Transfer or Check), and add a reference number or notes if you like. The balance and status update automatically.
- **Refund:** appears when an order was paid more than it needs (for example after you removed items) or was cancelled after payment. Enter the amount, up to what can be given back. It is saved as a negative payment, so the paid amount, balance and status follow it. To handle a return, first **Edit** the order to remove the returned items, then record the refund.
- **Edit** or **Delete** the order.

### Finding orders

- Search by order number, customer or product.
- Filter by payment status and by business.
- Choose the **period** (All Time, Today, This Week, This Month, This Year or a Custom Range) with the chips under the business chips. The list only shows orders inside that period. The period is shared with Home and Reports, so changing it on one page changes it on the others.
- Sort by newest date or by highest net sales.

## Receivables and expenses

These work the same in the app and on the web, and share the same data.

### Receivables

On **Home**, under Outstanding Receivables, tap **By customer**. The page lists who owes you, biggest balance first, with each unpaid order and how old it is. The totals at the top are split by age: 0-30, 31-60, 61-90 and over 90 days. For each customer you can:

- **WhatsApp:** open a chat with a ready-made reminder (it names the unpaid orders and the total). If the customer has a phone number saved it opens their chat; Philippine numbers written 09XXXXXXXXX are converted automatically.
- **Copy reminder:** copy the same message to paste anywhere.
- **Statement:** create the customer's statement PDF.

The page follows the business chosen on Home, not the period, and it uses your saved orders, so payments recorded in the app show up too.

### Expenses

Tap the **Expenses** card on Home (on the web you can also open `#/expenses`) to record what the business spends. Each expense has a date, a category (Supplies, Rent, Utilities, Salaries, Transport, Marketing, Equipment or Other), an amount, the business it belongs to (or General) and notes. The list follows the business and period chosen on Home and shows totals by category. General expenses count under All businesses only. Home shows **Expenses** and **Net Profit** (estimated profit minus the expenses of the same period); Net Profit is hidden while **Mask Profit Values** is on.

## Stock, restocks and barcodes

Open **Products** in the app (**Inventory** on the web) to keep track of how many units of each product you have. The app and the web share the same stock.

- **Stock:** tap **Stock** on a product and enter the units you have on hand now. From then on, every order (made on the web or in the app) takes its units off, a cancelled order gives them back, and restocks add units. Leave the number empty to stop tracking a product. Enter the same number again later to recount: the new number replaces the old count.
- **Low-stock level:** set the number at which you want a warning. The product shows **Low stock** in Inventory, appears under **Low Stock** on Home, and is out of stock at zero. The **Low stock** chip in Inventory shows only these products.
- **Restock:** tap **Restock** and enter how many units you bought, the cost per unit, the supplier and the date. By default the product's unit cost is set to the new cost, so future orders use it (turn the switch off to keep the old cost). The last five restocks are listed in the dialog, and you can delete a wrong one there.
- **Barcode:** type or scan a barcode into the product's Stock dialog. In a new order, tap **Scan** to add that product (scanning it again adds one more unit). In the app, **Scan** opens Google's barcode scanner (it needs Google Play services and no extra permission). On the web, camera scanning works in browsers that have a barcode reader, such as Chrome on Android. Elsewhere, such as iPhone Safari, type the code in the box, or use a USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner, which types the code for you.

Good to know: stock is counted from orders dated after your count, so orders you back-date to before the count are not taken off. Custom items (not picked from the product list) never use stock.

## Home dashboard

Home gives you a live summary of your business.

- **Business chips:** view everything together, or one business at a time.
- **Period chips:** All Time, Today, This Week, This Month, This Year or a **Custom Range** you pick.
- **Summary cards:**
  - Gross Sales, Discounts, Average Order and your Top Customer.
  - Net Sales and Estimated Profit (with the profit margin).
  - Collected Cash and Outstanding receivables.
- **Charts:** sales and profit trend, business comparison, product sales breakdown, and paid versus unpaid.
- **Reading the trend chart:** the bars follow the period you picked: months for This Year, All Time and long custom ranges, days for Today, This Week, This Month and short custom ranges (up to 62 days). Swipe sideways when there are many bars. Each bar has a short amount above it (12.4k means 12,400). The outlined bar beside it is the same time in the earlier period (last year for months, last week, last month, yesterday, or the range just before a custom one), and the arrow line shows the change in net sales. The month or day still in progress is in bold, and a month still running is compared with the same days of last year. If one bar is far taller than the rest, it is shortened and marked with a gap so the others stay readable. While profit is masked, the chart says "Profit hidden" and shows only net sales.
- **Outstanding Receivables:** orders with a balance, with quick **Details** and **Pay** buttons.
- **Recent Transactions:** your latest orders. Tap one to open it.

## Import and export

Open **Data Import** from your Profile page. Use the switch at the top to choose **Orders** or **Products**.

### Importing products

1. Choose the **business**. Every product in the file is added to that business.
2. Download a **CSV or Excel template** and fill it in, one product per row.
3. Tap **Choose CSV or Excel file** and pick your file.
4. Review the results: how many are new, how many already exist, and any problems listed by row.
5. Tap **Import** to confirm. Valid products are saved and uploaded to your cloud backup.

Columns:

- **Product Name** (required)
- **Selling Price** (required, a number of 0 or more)
- **Unit Cost** (optional, 0 or more; blank is saved as 0)
- **Active** (optional, Yes or No; blank means Yes)

Notes:

- Products with the same name already in that business are skipped. Turn on **Update existing products** to replace their price, cost and status instead.
- A name repeated inside the file is flagged, and only its first row is imported.
- Rows with problems are skipped; the rest are still imported.

### Importing orders

Download the orders template and fill in one row per product line. The required columns are Order Date, Customer, Business, Product, Quantity and Unit Price. Optional columns are Order Ref, Unit Cost, Discount %, Amount Paid, Payment Method and Remarks. Rows that share the same Order Ref become one order. You see a preview and any problems before anything is saved.

### Exporting orders

Use **Export** on the same screen to save your orders as a CSV file.

The web version has the same import and export. Orders and products you import there appear in the app, and the other way round.

## Profile and branding

Open **Profile** from your photo at the top right.

- **Authenticated identity:** your Google account, managed by Google.
- **Business Branding & Logo:** your business name, tagline, owner, contact number, email, address, website, social page and an optional TIN. Add your logo here.

Tap **Save Profile** to apply. If you leave with unsaved changes, the app asks whether to keep editing or discard.

The app and the web version use the same business profile and logo. Change it in one place and it appears in the other, and the PDFs from both use it.

## Cloud backup and sync

Your data is stored on your phone and backed up to your own account in the cloud.

- **When you sign in:** a loading screen appears while your backup is restored. If the connection is slow, the app continues with the data on your phone and syncs later.
- **Sync to Cloud:** on the **About** page, tap this to upload everything now. The status (Cloud Synced, Syncing to Cloud, Saved Locally (Offline) or Sync Error) is shown there and on your Profile.
- **Live updates:** while the app is open, changes made on the web or on another phone appear within a few seconds. They are also picked up when the app starts and when you come back to it.
- **Working offline:** you can keep recording sales without internet. Changes are saved on the phone and uploaded when you are back online.
- **What is shared:** businesses, products, customers, orders and payments, quotations, the profit mask setting and your business profile and logo. If two devices change the same record, the most recent change wins.
- **Deleted records:** when you delete something, other devices are told so it disappears there too. The small deletion notes are cleaned up from the cloud after about three months.
- **Logging out:** the app first backs up your data to the cloud, then clears this phone. Signing in again restores it. If the backup can't finish, the app tells you why before logging out.

> Tip: tap **Sync to Cloud** after a busy day, before changing phones.

## Security and privacy

- **Biometric lock:** on your Profile page, turn on the lock to require your fingerprint, face or device PIN each time you open the app.
- **Mask profit values:** hide profit amounts on screen (see Businesses above).
- **Your data:** each account only sees its own records. Logging out removes the data from the phone.
- **Web privacy notice:** the web version asks once whether it may use Google Analytics to count visits. It is off unless you allow it, it never receives your sales, customers or other business records, and you can change your choice any time under **Settings**, **About**, **Privacy**. The Android app does not use analytics.

## Using the web version

You can also use aMACEzing Sales in a browser on Windows, Linux, Mac, iPhone, iPad or any other device: open `https://rmrkweb.github.io/amacezing-sales-web/` and sign in with the same Google account. It looks and works like the app, and it widens to use a tablet or computer screen.

- **What you can do on the web:** almost everything the app does: Home, Sales, Inventory, Orders (quotations), Customers, Settings, Reports, Data Import and Export, and your Profile and branding. You can add, edit and delete records, create and edit orders, record payments, convert quotations to orders, and create PDF quotations and receipts to download or share.
- **Changes sync both ways.** A change made on the web reaches the app within a few seconds while the app is open, and the app picks it up when it starts or when you come back to it. A change made in the app shows on the web as soon as the app has uploaded it.
- **Deleting** an order, product, customer or quotation on one device removes it on the other devices too.
- **Editing an order:** you can change the products, quantities, prices, discount and remarks, and you can cancel the order. The customer, date and recorded payments stay as they are; use **Pay** to add a payment.
- **Working offline:** the web version opens without a connection once you have used it online. Changes are saved on your device and sent when you are back online.

- **Only in the app:** the biometric lock, Check for Updates and Sync to Cloud (the web version syncs by itself).

> Tip: orders made on the web are numbered with a W (ENG-2026-W0001) and the app's orders stay ENG-2026-0001, so orders made offline on both can never clash. Two phones on the same account that are both offline can still give the same number.

## Installing the web version as an app

You can put the web version on your home screen so it opens full screen like an app.

- **Android phones and computers (Chrome or Edge):** tap the **install** icon next to the **?** icon at the top, then tap **Install** in the box that appears.
- **iPhone and iPad:** tap the Share button in your browser, choose **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**. The install icon at the top shows the same steps.
- If you do not see the install icon, the web version may already be installed, or your browser does not support installing.

## Questions and troubleshooting

### "You are using an unsupported version"

The app couldn't confirm it is up to date, or an update was cancelled. Check your internet connection and tap **Try again**. If an update is offered, tap **Update now** and finish the install.

### The install is blocked

Android asks you to allow installs from this app once. Turn on **Allow from this source** in the settings screen that opens, go back, and tap **Install**.

### My data isn't showing after signing in

Wait for the "Syncing your data" screen to finish. If you were offline, connect to the internet, then open **About** and tap **Sync to Cloud**.

### I can't create an order, quotation or product

Add a business first: **Settings**, then **Businesses**.

### My import has problems

Read the list of problems under the summary. Each one names the row and what to fix. Fix the file and choose it again.

### I can't sign in on my iPhone

Open the web version in Safari (not inside another app) and turn off Private Browsing. Allow pop-ups for the site if your browser asks. If sign-in does not finish in the home-screen app, use the web version in Safari instead; your data is the same.

### The install icon is missing

The install icon only shows in browsers that can install the web version, and only while it is not installed yet. In Chrome or Edge, open the site in the browser itself (not inside another app). On iPhone, use Safari's Share button.

### The web version shows old data

Pull to refresh by reloading the page. The web version keeps a copy on your device so it opens offline; it updates as soon as it is online again.

### The guide won't load

The guide is loaded from the internet. Check your connection and tap the refresh icon. If you are offline, the app shows the last saved copy when it has one.
